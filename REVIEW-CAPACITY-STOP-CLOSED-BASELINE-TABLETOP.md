# Review-Capacity Stop Closed-Baseline Tabletop

Status: working synthetic research; not an operating threshold, gate, role, or eligibility decision.

## Question

Can the closed founder-only baseline distinguish a review queue that may remain
legible from one that must stop new hypothetical intake before correction,
provenance, or authority state becomes ambiguous?

## Construction and limits

This is a finite paper tabletop over fictional draft records. It uses no
person, account, contribution, capacity measurement, acceptance process, or
real workload. It does not establish a safe queue size or actual review
capacity.

| Case | Synthetic condition | Required closed-baseline result | Why continuation is refused or bounded |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | One labelled draft; its source list, scope, and correction owner remain visible. | No inference about capacity; preserve as a single analytical record. | A readable record is not evidence that a future role may be opened. |
| Q2 | A second draft arrives while the first has an unresolved provenance discrepancy. | Stop hypothetical intake; preserve the discrepancy and first-record boundary. | New work would make the correction state and acceptance meaning ambiguous. |
| Q3 | A revision changes a draft's claim class while the earlier review reason is unresolved. | Stop or route the changed question as separately governed. | The queue cannot silently convert review delay into scope consent or decision voice. |
| Q4 | Several drafts have recorded timestamps but no reasoned disposition or correction route. | Stop hypothetical intake; treat timestamps as insufficient. | Counting items cannot stand in for review, correction, or accountable capacity. |
| Q5 | A hypothetical pause is requested after a public-history or attribution concern appears. | Stop new intake and preserve a visible no-reversal record. | Prospective stopping cannot erase reliance, knowledge, or attributable creation. |

## Result

The construction supports one narrow negative control: a future gate cannot
use queue size, elapsed time, or a clean repository as a proxy for review
capacity. Any unresolved provenance, correction, scope, authority, or
non-reversible-consequence state requires prospective stopping in the
synthetic baseline. What would count as timely review, adequate capacity, or a
legitimate appeal remains unknown.

## Disposition

Analytical advance only. Output 6 and the first eligibility gate remain
Partial and not decision-ready. Rights, actual capacity evidence, thresholds,
and a future-role design remain outside this tabletop.
