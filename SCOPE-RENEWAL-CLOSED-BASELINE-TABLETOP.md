# Scope-Renewal Closed-Baseline Tabletop

Status: **synthetic analytical evidence - not an active role, consent process,
gate, decision, or activation**

## Question

When a hypothetical bounded research request changes, which changes may remain
inside the same analytical scope, which would require fresh bilateral opt-in,
and which must stop because the current founder-only baseline cannot supply a
legitimate continuation path?

This tabletop tests the current closed baseline only. Joe remains the sole
eligible actor for authoritative effort, decision voice, and meta-governance
voice. A "fresh opt-in" result names evidence a future gate would need; it
does not create a person, a role, terms, an invitation, or a right to continue.

## Evidence Boundary

The tabletop uses only repository-owned Phase 0 records:

- `CONSTITUTION.md`
- `GOVERNANCE.md`
- `RESEARCH.md`
- `FIRST-ELIGIBILITY-GATE-EVIDENCE-SPECIFICATION.md`
- `CONSENT-EXIT-REVIEW-APPEAL-FAILURE-CASE-MATRIX.md`
- `PHASE-0-GRADUATION-PACKET.md`

No person, account, agreement, employment or contractor relationship, live
work, private source, participant data, incentive, legal opinion, publication,
or external system is used. The fixtures cannot establish actual
comprehension, consent, enforceability, review capacity, rights, or
reversibility.

## Construction and Resolution Method

The baseline fixture is a hypothetical, unactivated research draft limited to
one named public-source question. Each subsequent row changes exactly one
material dimension. It is resolved by the current authority hierarchy and the
Phase 0 evidence specification:

- **continue in analytical scope** means the fixture has not changed a
  material boundary; it does not authorize any real work;
- **fresh bilateral opt-in required** means a future gate would need a new,
  recorded project-and-candidate consent decision before a changed fixture
  could be considered; and
- **stop / separately governed** means the change crosses a boundary that the
  closed baseline cannot cure through renewal alone.

Every row also records what prospective stopping cannot undo. No classification
is a legal conclusion or an operational consent term.

## Claim Ledger

- **Established here:** the current repository has no non-Joe effort
  eligibility and distinguishes effort from both forms of voice.
- **Synthetic support:** material changes in question, source, claim,
  disposition, review burden, or attribution create identifiable renewal or
  stop boundaries in the tabletop.
- **Unknown:** valid disclosure language, who may qualify, evidence quality,
  review capacity, rights, enforceability, remedies, portability, and real
  participant understanding.
- **Not claimed:** an active role, consent, a sufficient gate, a safe
  continuation protocol, real reversibility, legal sufficiency, or Phase 0
  readiness.

## Results

The tabletop finds that a named question alone is not a durable consent
boundary. A future gate would need to recognize at least five material change
classes: question, source/provenance, claim or acceptance state, review time
and load, and withdrawal/public-history consequences.

The closed baseline supplies no continuation path for any hypothetical person:
Joe's current sole eligibility means every non-Joe fixture remains inactive.
The renewal classification is therefore a negative design finding, not a
permission. It identifies evidence that must exist before a future gate could
even consider a changed request.

Two changes cannot be repaired by fresh consent alone. A request for project
representation or external contact crosses a separately governed external and
authority boundary; a change from draft to project-supported claim requires a
separate acceptance path and Joe's authority. Withdrawal after public history
also demonstrates that prospective stopping is containment, not restoration.

## Negative and No-Go Evidence

A future effort gate should remain unsatisfied when:

- the task schema cannot distinguish a material scope change from a cosmetic
  edit;
- a new source, model-assisted input, or pre-existing material cannot be
  reconstructed and reviewed for provenance and rights;
- renewed work depends on an unrecorded request, social pressure, or a review
  backlog that obscures a meaningful ability to decline;
- a revision would cause a draft to be treated as project decision, policy, or
  representation without the required authority path; or
- access stopping, withdrawal, or repository correction is represented as
  erasing public history, reliance, attribution, or retained creation.

## Disposition and Next Test Boundary

Current disposition: **synthetic analytical advance; still not decision-ready**.

This result makes the consent/review/appeal evidence bundle more explicit but
does not resolve documentation/specification licensing, contribution
provenance, patent posture, actual review capacity, operational disclosures,
or enforceable exit and portability rights. The next owner-local test should
be a review-capacity stop tabletop using a synthetic queue, while the
source-bounded rights and provenance comparison remains a hard dependency
before any gate could be proposed.
