# Joint-Creation Provenance Closed-Baseline Tabletop

Status: working synthetic research; not a rights posture, contribution rule, gate, role, or eligibility decision.

## Question

Can the closed founder-only baseline keep a model-assisted draft with pre-existing material from being treated as accepted or portable creation when provenance and attribution are disputed?

## Construction and limits

This finite paper tabletop uses fictional draft records only. It supplies no person, contribution, account, license selection, legal conclusion, patent posture, or actual attribution process. It does not establish that a future contribution can be accepted safely.

| Case | Synthetic condition | Required closed-baseline result | Why acceptance or portability stops |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1a | A draft names model assistance only after an apparent acceptance record exists. | Freeze the claimed acceptance state; preserve the disclosure discrepancy and require provenance re-review. | Late disclosure can change source, authorship, conflict, and correction meaning. |
| P1b | A draft combines a pre-existing fragment with a new claim but cannot identify their boundary. | Preserve both the uncertainty and the source gap; do not treat the combined draft as attributable creation. | A readable merged file is not a provenance ledger. |
| J1a | A project edit and a hypothetical outside edit are interleaved without a contribution or acceptance-state boundary. | Stop further joint editing; preserve the disputed history and require a separately governed fork or correction route. | History alone does not identify portable creation or rights. |
| J1b | A model-assisted revision is attributed to one hypothetical contributor while a conflicting attribution claim remains unresolved. | Keep the attribution disputed and refuse downstream reliance or acceptance. | Neither model use nor a label resolves authorship, rights, or responsibility. |

## Result

The construction supports one narrow negative control: acceptance, portability, and attribution must remain stopped whenever the record cannot reconstruct source, pre-existing material, model assistance, contribution boundary, and acceptance state. A repository commit, disclosure label, or clean rollback cannot repair a disputed retained-creation consequence by itself.
