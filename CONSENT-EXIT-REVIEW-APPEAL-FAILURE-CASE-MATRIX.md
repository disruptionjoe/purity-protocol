# Consent, Exit, Review, and Appeal Failure-Case Matrix

Status: **working analytical research - not active terms, a gate, a proposal,
a decision, or activation**

Authority: this Lane 1 artifact pressure-tests the hypothetical bounded-effort
role in `FIRST-ELIGIBILITY-GATE-EVIDENCE-SPECIFICATION.md`. It does not make
that role real, make anyone eligible, settle rights, establish legal
conclusions, satisfy evidence, or commit Joe to considering or approving a
gate.

## Research Question

Where can a hypothetical bounded research-effort relationship fail across
consent, review, rejection, correction, appeal, exit, and attributable
creation even when repository access can be stopped?

The question matters because Purity Protocol treats software rollback, access
revocation, data restoration, economic reversal, reputational repair,
governance reversal, and restoration of consent as distinct capabilities.

## Evidence Boundary

This analysis uses only current Purity Protocol authority and research
surfaces:

- `CONSTITUTION.md`
- `GOVERNANCE.md`
- `RESEARCH.md`
- `FIRST-ELIGIBILITY-GATE-EVIDENCE-SPECIFICATION.md`
- `PHASE-0-GRADUATION-PACKET.md`

No outside contributor, participant, source, legal opinion, employment or
contract model, live work, personal data, incentive, account, deployment, or
field evidence is used. The cases are synthetic constructions. They cannot
establish how a real person, court, platform, or institution would behave.

## Construction Choices

- The hypothetical subject is the specification's bounded research
  contributor, not an active role.
- Effort eligibility is held separate from decision and meta-governance voice.
- Every case distinguishes detection, immediate containment, prospective
  stopping, correction, and consequences that cannot be assumed reversible.
- A case passes analysis only when it preserves Joe's current authority,
  bilateral opt-in, source provenance, negative evidence, and explicit
  uncertainty.
- A clean repository rollback is never accepted as proof that consent,
  knowledge, reliance, reputation, rights, or joint creation were restored.

## Claim Ledger

- **Established here:** Joe alone is currently eligible for authoritative

- **Synthetic support:** the cases below expose distinct failure paths and

- **Unknown:** enforceable rights, licensing, patent posture, actual review

## Cross-Case Results

### Consent is a continuing boundary

**Synthetic support:** a one-time opt-in cannot cover later scope expansion,
new claim classes, public reliance, changed rights assumptions, or new
consequences. A future gate would need observable consent renewal points.

This does not establish an active consent process.

### Review and appeal can create authority by implication

**Synthetic support:** accepting drafts, requesting consequential revisions, or
hearing appeals can blur effort into decision or meta-governance voice even
when no formal vote exists. A future gate would need to distinguish evidence
or process challenge from policy authority while preserving a correction path.

This does not establish a legitimate appeal design.

### Exit is prospective containment, not erasure

**Synthetic support:** stopping access or new work can limit future effects but
cannot be assumed to reverse knowledge, public history, reliance, granted
rights, reputation, joint creation, or third-party copies.

This does not establish which rights or remedies apply.

### Metabolic capacity is part of consent integrity

**Synthetic support:** accepting bounded effort without demonstrated review,
correction, provenance, and appeal capacity can create misleading consent and
founder dependence. A future role should remain a no-go if the repository
cannot stop intake before those loads compound.

No capacity threshold is established here.

   that the existing hierarchy resolves public visibility, licensing, merge,

2. **Scope-renewal tabletop:** executed as a closed-baseline synthetic control
   in `SCOPE-RENEWAL-CLOSED-BASELINE-TABLETOP.md`. It resolves cosmetic edits,
   changed questions, sources, claim states, model assistance, review delay,
   and withdrawal into continuation, fresh hypothetical opt-in, or a separate
   stop/governance boundary. It establishes no active consent process or role.
3. **Exit-with-public-history tabletop:** apply E1 through E3 to synthetic
   access, merged-draft, and attribution states; require separate containment
   and non-reversible consequence records.
4. **Review-capacity stop tabletop:** executed as a closed-baseline synthetic
   control in `REVIEW-CAPACITY-STOP-CLOSED-BASELINE-TABLETOP.md`. It refuses
   to treat queue size, elapsed time, or a clean repository as capacity
   evidence and stops hypothetical intake when provenance, correction, scope,
   authority, or non-reversible-consequence state is unresolved.
5. **Joint-creation provenance tabletop:** executed as a closed-baseline
   synthetic control in `JOINT-CREATION-PROVENANCE-CLOSED-BASELINE-TABLETOP.md`.
   It refuses acceptance, portability, or attribution conclusions when source,
   pre-existing material, model assistance, contribution boundary, or acceptance
   state cannot be reconstructed.

Each future test would need explicit fixtures, pass/fail conditions,
construction limits, and preserved no-go results. None should use a real
contributor, account, incentive, private source, or external action.

## Negative and No-Go Evidence

A future bounded-effort gate should remain unsatisfied when:

- authoritative effort cannot stay distinct from decision or
  meta-governance voice;
- disclosure, review, rejection, correction, appeal, or exit depends on
  rights the project has not adopted or cannot honor;
- stopping is represented as erasing public history, knowledge, reliance,
  reputation, or jointly created consequences;
- provenance or attribution cannot be reconstructed well enough to correct or
  fork the work;
- reviewer concentration or backlog prevents timely, reasoned disposition;
  or
- resolving a case would require live participants, hidden authority, legal
  certainty the repository lacks, or an unapproved external action.
