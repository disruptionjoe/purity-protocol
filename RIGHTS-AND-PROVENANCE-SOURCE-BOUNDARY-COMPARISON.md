# Rights and Provenance Source-Boundary Comparison

Status: **bounded external-support research — not a recommendation, legal
advice, decision, license, contribution rule, or activation**

## Question and Boundary

What decision-relevant differences can a small primary-source comparison make
legible before Purity Protocol chooses any documentation/specification license
or contribution-provenance posture for a possible future bounded-research
role?

This comparison uses only the named primary texts below. It records what each
text says within its own scope and the questions it does not answer for Purity
Protocol. It does not interpret law, determine compatibility, establish
ownership, select a license, create terms for a contributor, or change the
repository's current founder-only state. The repository's existing MIT license
for code remains its own limited decision and does not answer this question.

## Disconfirming Conditions and Open Return

The evidence remains **Unknown** and no future gate may rely on it if any of
the following is true:

- the proposed material boundary cannot distinguish code, documentation,
  specifications, pre-existing work, joint work, and third-party material;
- a licensing or provenance mechanism is used to imply authority, eligibility,
  representation, ownership, patent rights, or real reversibility that its
  source does not establish;
- the project cannot preserve an accurate, correctable provenance record
  without collecting more personal information or making stronger promises
  than it can govern; or
- the result would be treated as legal advice, a contribution program, a role,
  a gate, or an activation rather than an input for Joe's later decision.

The next bounded return is not another license comparison by default. It is a
separate decision on whether to seek appropriate legal review and, if so, what
specific material and contribution boundary that review must address. Until
then, no non-Joe person is eligible to contribute authoritative effort,
decision voice, or meta-governance voice.
